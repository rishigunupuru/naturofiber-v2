About NaturoFiber.

NaturoFiber was founded by two faculty members of the Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani.

Founders

Engineers first.

Co-Founder & Director

Prof. Shamsher Bahadur Singh

Shri S. K. Birla Chair & Senior Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani

Ph.D. (IIT Kanpur) · P.E. (Michigan, USA) · C.Eng. · Fellow ASCE, SEI, ICE (UK), CDRI, IAStructE, IE · ACI Committees 440 & 423 · BIS CED 54

Advanced composite materials, FRP structures, functionally graded composites and finite-element modelling. Leads technology vision, structural FRP architecture and product validation.

Co-Founder & Director

Dr. G. Muthukumar

Associate Professor, Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani

Ph.D. & M.E., Structural Engineering (BITS Pilani)

Earthquake-resistant design of concrete structures, fire resistance and nonlinear finite-element analysis. Leads structural analysis, prototype assessment and product development.

Incubation

NaturoFiber is incubated at PIEDS, BITS Pilani, with its research carried out in the composites and structures laboratories of the Department of Civil Engineering.
